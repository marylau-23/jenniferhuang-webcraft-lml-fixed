About

Liang Minliang(梁敏亮) Design Studio (LML)™  🤘 merges business with art, using cutting-edge technology to craft unique digital experiences that give brands digital vitality and lasting impact🔖.

Full-stack Designer👻 Not just a Designer, annnd also a Developer

I'm Liang Minliang (梁敏亮, born 1988, Year of the Loong), from Xinhui, Guangdong, China, a full-stack visual designer with over 20 years of experience. Design is an emotional brush of light and pixels, development is a rational language of symbols and algorithms. I combine a designer’s aesthetic sensibility with a programmer’s logical thinking, working across the full process—from art direction and brand building to web interaction and technical implementation—to ensure that every detail precisely conveys the spirit of the brand. I believe that outstanding work comes from the ability to build end to end—an approach that goes beyond technology, rooted in a way of thinking that integrates aesthetics and function. At the intersection of form and code, I continue to explore solutions that are clear, elegant, and sustainable. I look forward to collaborating with you to create a world woven together by code and aesthetics.
